# Supplier sign-up is gated by legal review

Guests cannot register themselves as suppliers. The `legal_committee` must first accept a guest's legal information, then the `admin` creates the account and the `procurement_officer` sends the email verification (see **supplier_signup_process** in the glossary). We chose this so that every supplier is legally screened before they can take part in any tender, at the cost of a slower sign-up.
