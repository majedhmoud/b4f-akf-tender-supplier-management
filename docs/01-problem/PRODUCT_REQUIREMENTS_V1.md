# Product Requirements V1

The Aga Khan Foundation’s procurement department needs a web platform to manage tenders and suppliers.

- Publish tender announcements and terms of reference, receive sealed bids, and maintain bid confidentiality.
- Keep the legal, technical, and financial evaluation committees’ responsibilities separate to ensure transparency.
- Maintain a central history of supplier dealings and dynamically rate suppliers on work quality and compliance with terms to support bid screening and reduce operational risks.
