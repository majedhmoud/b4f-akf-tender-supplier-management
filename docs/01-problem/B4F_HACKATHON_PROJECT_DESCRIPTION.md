# Tender and Supplier Management Platform (Aga Khan Foundation)

An integrated web platform designed specifically for the Aga Khan Foundation’s procurement department automates the entire tender lifecycle. The process begins with a public tender announcement and publication of the terms of reference (ToR). The platform then receives sealed bids and keeps them confidential. It manages the three evaluation committees (legal, technical, and financial) separately to ensure transparency and prevent their authority from overlapping.

The platform also includes a central supplier database that records the Foundation’s history of dealings with each supplier. A dynamic rating system assesses each supplier’s performance based on the quality of their work and their compliance with the terms. This makes it easier to screen bids and reduces operational risks.
